About

I started my hair journey through training and a passion for making people look beautiful. I have been doing hair since 2019, and over the years I have grown my skills in many hairstyling techniques.

I am proud of creating neat, beautiful hairstyles, including braids, cornrows, wig making and installation, frontal installation, bridal hairstyling and hair revamping. I also love sharing what I know by training others.

I want every client to feel comfortable, welcomed and relaxed, and confident that her hair is in good hands. At Boluweave Matrix, I want you to leave feeling beautiful, satisfied and happy with how you look.
